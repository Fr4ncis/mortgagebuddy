What each amount buys you

Assumes your contractual monthly payment stays the same after overpaying (so the saving comes off the term, not the payment) and the rate holds at what you entered. Interest is charged monthly on the balance. The last payment is nearly always a part-payment — the days/hours/minutes come from how far into that final month the debt clears, which is what makes small overpayments visible at all.

Check your lender's rules. Some charge an early repayment fee, or cap penalty-free overpayments at a percentage of the balance each year. Many treat a large overpayment as a capital repayment and respond by cutting your monthly payment rather than your term — which is the opposite of what this calculator assumes, and wipes out the time saving. If you want the term shortened, say so explicitly. Estimates only, not advice.
